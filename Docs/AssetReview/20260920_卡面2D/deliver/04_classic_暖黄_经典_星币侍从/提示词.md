## 3. 英文提示词（主稿，整段粘贴）

```
Create one vertical portrait card illustration (about 1:1.43 portrait - a 66 x 94 mm card that fits a 70 x 99 mm card sleeve) for an AR pet game. This is the "Classic" card of a four-card tarot-frog set. It will be printed matte and recognized by a phone camera from grayscale frames, so the artwork must keep a bold graphic silhouette and an even, all-over texture.

Style: flat woodcut 2D illustration in the spirit of Marseille tarot - large flat color fields, thick black outlines, crisp hard edges. No realistic shading, no gradients, no glow, no blur, no depth of field, no painterly rendering. Absurd cartoon proportions: big round head, chubby pear-shaped body. Retro screen-print texture crossed with modern flat design. Every large color field carries a subtle all-over texture of stippling, fine hatching, tiny stars or small leaves; the texture stays within plus/minus 5% brightness, varies in size, angle and density, never falls into a regular repeat, and no large blank patch is left untextured. Every edge is razor sharp - no haze, no film grain, no noise.

Subject - the character is a fixed design, copy it exactly: reproduce the original character shown in Image 2, a chubby toy-like 3D-rendered mascot nicknamed Naiwa. It is NOT a generic frog and NOT a realistic frog; do not redesign or "improve" it. One continuous bell-pear silhouette: a wide rounded dome head with no neck that merges directly into an oversized round body; the huge round belly is the widest point of the whole figure, tapering down into very short stubby arms and legs with no visible elbows or knees. Hands and feet are a contrasting pale tan / cream color, clearly different from the body hue - mitten-like paws with three to four rounded finger hints, and broad flat feet with three separate rounded toe lobes, no webbing. Two large dome-shaped eyes sit on the top-front of the head, very close together near the midline and protruding slightly above the head outline, each with a pale yellow-green iris and one very large near-black round pupil filling most of the eye. The mouth is a small gentle closed smile - a short dark line across the lower face with both corners turned slightly up, fully closed, giving a calm good-natured look. The skin is matte clay-like with soft diffuse shading only - no wet specular highlights, no warts, no bumps. This variant's skin is warm yellow; its belly is the large, almost circular cream-white patch with a crisp hard edge and a thin darker outline. No eyebrows, eyelashes, blush, tail, markings, clothing or accessories anywhere on the character.

Pose, from Image 1 (Page of Pentacles): the frog stands upright in a field, both arms raised, holding one large gold coin up beside its head, looking up at it with an honest, good-natured gentle smile.

Symbols: the large gold coin carries only one simple geometric star shape, with no letters or writing; a cloth satchel is slung across the frog's body; a few small flowers are scattered at its feet; low shrubs and ploughed-field texture run along the distance.

Composition: the frog stands centered, filling the middle of the card, head and raised coin above the middle line. Fill the upper and lower zones with the field texture and the coin shape - no untextured blank areas.

Frame: vertical; an outer band of fine low-contrast ornament; inside it a double-outlined tarot frame (thick outer line, thin inner line, small corner flourishes, total frame width under 3% of the card width); inside the frame sits the illustration; the bottom fifth of the card is a quiet, finely textured title band with no key elements, separated from the illustration by a thin horizontal rule or a branch; a small empty plaque at the top center. The frog's face and the key symbols stay out of the outer 8% border band.

Tonal design, and full color is mandatory: the background is a dark grey ground at roughly 24-31% brightness, NOT the bright yellow of the original scan; it is clearly darker than the frog's body at 78-86%, a difference of at least 50 points. The belly is 15-25% brighter than the body; eyes and limbs each sit at their own brightness step. The output must be a vivid, fully colored illustration - it must not be a black-and-white, grayscale, monochrome or line-sketch image, and the warm-yellow skin must stay clearly visible.

Text: render no text of any kind anywhere in the image - no letters, words, numbers, roman numerals, titles or watermarks. The title band and the top plaque stay completely empty. The gold coin carries only the geometric star, no letters.

References: Image 1 is the 1909 Rider-Waite tarot scan of the Page of Pentacles - take only the centered upright pose, the raised-object gesture and the vertical centered composition; do not copy its bright yellow background field, its clothing, realistic line shading or paper tone. Image 2 is our own 3D render of this exact character - copy the character's silhouette, proportions, colors, eye design and hand-and-foot design from it exactly; where any wording in this prompt conflicts with Image 2, Image 2 wins; never substitute a generic frog. Do not take its pose from Image 2.
```

## 4. 中文对照（供审阅，内容与主稿一致）

```
创作一张竖版卡片插画（约 1:1.43 竖版；成品 66×94 mm，适配 70×99 mm 内径卡片保护套），用于 AR 萌宠游戏。这是"塔罗奶蛙"四张套卡中的"经典"卡。卡片将哑光打印并由手机摄像头从灰度画面中识别，因此画面必须轮廓醒目、纹理满幅铺匀。
风格：马赛塔罗精神的木刻平涂 2D 插画——大色块平涂、粗黑描边、干净硬边。不要写实光影、不要渐变、不要发光、不要模糊、不要景深、不要手绘渲染。荒诞卡通比例：大圆头、圆胖梨形身体。复古丝网印刷质感 × 现代扁平设计。每一块大色域都铺一层若隐若现的满幅细纹（点刻、细排线、小星点或小叶片）；纹样明暗波动控制在 ±5% 以内，大小、角度、疏密都有变化，不得规则重复，不得留下无纹样的大块空白。所有边缘锐利——不要雾化、不要颗粒噪点。
主体——角色是定死的设计，严格照图复刻：完全复现图 2 中的原创角色（外号"奶蛙"的圆胖玩偶感 3D 渲染形象）。它不是普通青蛙、也不是写实青蛙；不要重新设计、不要"改良"。整块钟形/梨形轮廓：宽圆穹顶头部与身体直接相连、没有脖子；巨大的圆腹是全身最宽处，向下收成极短的四肢，看不到肘和膝。手脚是与体色明显不同的浅米色/奶油色——连指圆掌（3~4 根指头暗示）+ 宽扁脚，脚上有 3 个各自独立的圆润脚趾瓣，无蹼。两只大 dome 眼位于头顶正面、靠近中线、微微凸出头顶轮廓，淡黄绿虹膜 + 占满大半个眼的极大近黑圆瞳。嘴是横贯面部下沿的一条短弧线、完全闭合、两端微微上扬，是一个恬静的微笑——不要夸张咧嘴，也不要画成平直线。皮肤是哑光黏土质感、只有柔和漫射——不要湿反光、不要疣粒。本款肤色为暖黄；肚皮是大块近圆形奶油白斑、与体色清晰硬边分界并带一圈略深描边。角色身上不要眉毛、睫毛、腮红、尾巴、花纹、衣物或配饰。
姿态（取自图 1 星币侍从）：青蛙全身挺立在田野中，双臂上举，在头侧托起一枚大金币，抬头注视着它，面带老实敦厚的恬静微笑。
符号：大金币上只刻一个简单的几何星形、无字无字母；身上斜挎一只布袋；脚下散落几朵小花；远处是低矮树丛与耕地纹理。
构图：青蛙居中、撑满画面中部，头与高举的金币越过中线。上方与下方空隙用田野纹理和金币形状填满——不留无纹样空白。
边框：竖版；最外一圈低对比细纹装饰带；其内双层描边塔罗框（外粗内细、四角小花饰，框总宽不超过卡面 3%）；框内为插画；底部约 1/5 是安静的细纹标题带、不放主要元素，用一条细横线或横枝与插画分隔；顶部中央留一小块空白牌位。蛙脸与关键符号避开四周约 8% 边带。
明暗设计，且必须全彩：背景为深灰底、约 24–31% 亮度——不要原稿那种明黄大底；明显暗于蛙身（78–86%），差值至少 50 个点；肚皮比身体亮 15–25%；眼睛、四肢各成一档。成品必须是鲜艳的全彩插画——不得是黑白稿、灰度稿、单色稿或线稿，暖黄皮肤必须清晰可见。
文字：画面中不得出现任何文字——字母、单词、数字、罗马数字、标题、水印一律不要；标题带与顶部牌位保持完全空白；金币上只允许几何星形，不得出现字母。
参考图：图 1 是 1909 年 Rider-Waite 塔罗原稿"星币侍从"——只取其居中挺立姿态、举物手势与竖版居中构图，不得照抄其明黄大底、服装、写实排线与纸色；图 2 是这个角色的本人 3D 渲染——轮廓、比例、配色、眼睛画法、手脚画法全部严格照它复刻，本提示词任何文字与图 2 冲突时以图 2 为准，绝不替换成普通青蛙；姿势不得从图 2 取。
```

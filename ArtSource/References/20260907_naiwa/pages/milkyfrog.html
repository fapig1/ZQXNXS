/ 角色档案/ Character File

他是一只圆滚滚的、
笑起来停不下来的蛙。 A round little frog
who simply cannot stop laughing.

奶蛙没什么大事要说,也不讲什么道理。他唯一会做的就是——你点他一下,他就笑给你看。捧着肚子笑、抖着身子笑、笑到喘不上气、最后笑晕过去。是互联网时代的电子木鱼,也是你口袋里的快乐制造机。 Milky Frog has nothing important to say. The only thing he does is laugh — when you tap him. Belly-clutching, full-body-shaking, can't-catch-his-breath laughter. He's a cyber wooden fish, a pocket-sized joy generator for the internet age.
